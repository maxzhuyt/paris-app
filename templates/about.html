Overview

PARIS stands for Police AI Report & Incident Simulator — a research tool for studying how AI systems generate police incident reports from bodycam video. It supports two pipelines: a transcript-based pipeline (Whisper + DeepSeek-Reasoner) and a direct video-analysis pipeline (Gemini 2.5 Pro). Researchers can run both side-by-side, configure prompts and parameters, and export results for analysis.
